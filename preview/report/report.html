Evidence before interpretation

How to Read This Report

Three independent signal families create the market-signal label. Context, supplier leads and client compliance checks remain separate.

Meta advertising
Looks for sustained, relevant activity across several advertisers. A positive result requires both activity and age thresholds; a visible ad is not enough by itself.
Marketplace
Looks for an approved Amazon list signal or relevant cross-platform visibility with a top-ten position. Position is evidence of visibility, not a sales count.
Google search
Compares recent and prior four-week averages. A positive result requires both the growth ratio and the absolute increase to pass.

Signal label

Strong means 3 positive core signals; Promising 2; Worth Watching 1; Weak 0.

Coverage

Available tells you what could be assessed. Missing evidence is not a negative result.

Weekly status

New, Returning, Strengthened and Weakened compare only with a comparable prior report.

Coverage Changed

Source availability changed, so no upward or downward movement is claimed.

Quick commerce

Nine platform-city checks provide availability and competition context only.

Supplier leads

Operator-reviewed decision support

Method and Client Responsibility

The service reduces research effort and makes evidence inspectable. Final commercial, supplier, safety and compliance decisions remain with the client.

Five-step method

  1. Discover and normalise candidate product concepts across the approved India categories.
  2. Check Meta advertising, marketplace visibility and Google search as three separate core signal families.
  3. Apply deterministic thresholds, preserve missing coverage, and rank eligible opportunities without weak filler.
  4. Add quick-commerce context, lightweight client-review flags, and possible supplier leads after selection.
  5. Review the checked data and regenerate both the PDF and Excel from the same source before delivery.

Client responsibility

The client—not NeuraFlow—must:

  • decide which products, if any, deserve further testing;
  • verify product compliance, warnings, age guidance and claims;
  • contact and assess supplier identity, stock, terms and suitability;
  • sample product quality, packaging, delivery and returns;
  • calculate landed cost, pricing, margin and commercial risk independently.
Supplier boundary. Possible supplier leads have not been contacted, audited, recommended or verified. They are starting points for independent investigation.
Report-use boundary. This briefing is decision support. It is not a guarantee of demand, sales, compliance, supplier suitability, product performance or profitability.
