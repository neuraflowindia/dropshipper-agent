## R01. Search allowance, ordering and saved-reply recovery

- `execute_source_run` checks both pass capacity and the original shared turn before a fresh-work resolver. The12-source/10-model defaults are per-turn scheduling sizes, not a total-job allowance or provider invoice. Older turns retain their original grants.
- Existing spent/closed prepared runs skip fresh question preparation, but retain current-description validation, original-reply recovery, cursor advancement and later stages. Missing runs and genuine later turns prepare their own questions normally.
- Ordinary source inspection visits at most64 entries, with a cooperative60-second yield checked **between completed entries**. Preparation and one local operation can exceed60 seconds. A separate priority scan selects at most8 saved replies; spent-turn saved-only inspection has its own64-entry bound. These bounds are not one combined64-entry ceiling.
- Full-run and subset cursors remain separate. Newly appended tasks cannot reset an unfinished frozen prefix. Record each outcome immediately; preserve an unfinished traversal through readiness and omit the optional second parent visit after a yielded pass.
- Saved original application does not repeat transport or reserve another callback. A recovered supplier reply triggers its dependency planner once so discovered company pages survive; unchanged replay does not repeat planning. Ordinary preparation after the final genuine callback remains.
- One newly uncertain actual original can receive one original-only recovery inside the same open turn. Repeated unanswered recovery does not create another obligation. Future retry/access waits remain waits; an ordinary unfinished suffix can continue.
- Next-turn readiness checks the latest valid **closed** turn's actual model/source capacity and current due work, including protected lanes, one-slot alternation and indivisible carried batches. A new unused turn supersedes the old spent witness, preventing endless empty turns. Never reopen/reset an old turn or override Stop.
- Semantic progress excludes cursor movement and retry-only/diagnostic writes.
