Updated: 7 October 2026. This document specifies **how to measure** a bounded experiment/rehearsal. The [master implementation plan](execution_specification.md) owns execution order/mechanisms, the [product brief](../../../product/india_dropshipping_product_opportunity_radar_product_brief.md) owns delivery requirements, the [cost plan](../../build_activation_and_costs_2026-10-03.md) owns funded limits and the [benchmark record](benchmark_record.md) owns actual results. Do not duplicate those authorities here.

The isolated direct Amazon India Bestsellers trial is complete. The current owner-approved P2 block permits local ten-criterion implementation, saved-data migration, a bounded useful set of public-input AI reviews and an internal connected report sample. [Actual results](benchmark_record.md) distinguish fixtures, saved-data interpretation, source collection and live release. P2 permits no fresh source collections, deployment, approval, upload or publication, and is not an instruction to run the whole programme.

## 1. Measurement units

| Unit | Count separately |
|---|---|
| Logical research question | Family/phrase/source/purpose or named concept/list question; exact scope/options. |
| Physical acquisition | Actual browser/provider read/capture. Shared capture counted once; replay is not acquisition. |
| Source output | Returned parent ads/cards/listings/pages, usable/mapped/uninterpretable/partial/noise and cap truncation. |
| Concept | Functional identity after matching; source seller/colour/pack count is not concept count. |
| Screening | Finished checklist/relevance/market work versus not tried/pending/unavailable. |
| Recommendation supply | Eligible never-published New, eligible unpublished carryover, material updates, already queued and set aside. |
| Report | Distinct logical issue/revision; two languages are one issue, not two research costs. |
| Expense/effort | Collection fee, known read fee, unknown hold, direct-page attempts, model usage/time, supplier queries and review time. |

Always show denominator and checkpoint date. Keep collection success, semantic relevance, useful product yield, funds and report readiness distinct.

## 2. Immediate direct-Amazon trial protocol

The completed [section19](execution_specification.md#19-standalone-direct-amazon-experiment) trial used three inspected India departments and six corrected verification visits, retaining initial price-incomplete visits and the local startup failure. One page/up to100cards per attempt; observed30 per successful capture. No Apify/model calls or production writes. Reuse this bounded protocol for a separately authorised later source trial, not an automatic repeat after closeout.

Report by case/round:

1. Attempts and access-success ratio.
2. Correct-country/list-success ratio; challenge 200 is not success.
3. Captured/parsed/usable card counts and missing ASIN/price/photo/position fields.
4. Usable-capture/attempt ratio, elapsed time and exact partial/error reason.
5. Unique ASIN/title examples, not inferred functional product/pass yield.
6. Repeatability/replay/isolation checks; a same-day round does not prove weekly freshness.

No automatic promotion. Regroup with actual results; direct adapter integration is a distinct later block. Failed direct access can be useful diagnostic evidence without being a product-market negative.

## 3. Connected-source and interpretation measurements

For an approved coherent implementation block:

1. Inspect current actual route/input/control/schema, target purpose and existing equivalent captures/jobs.
2. Prepare a finite scope with item/action/model/request/spend/deadline limits. State the actual missing question and baseline.
3. Test relevant deterministic failure/replay paths before live credentials/network where risk warrants it.
4. Paid work reserves funds and durable intent under the planned exact-attempt/short-lock contract; direct work saves the actual acquisition identity. No fictitious paid attempt for a free browser capture.
5. Retain bounded original response/URL/scope/time/hash and parser status. Preserve partial/noise and omitted counts.
6. Review identity/relevance/checklist/supplier interpretations independently against actual material; validate references and record reasoned judgments, not schema-only pass.
7. Reconcile expense and retain unknowns. Count public zero-fee effort and skipped/not-applicable source checks.
8. Save Pass/Partial/Fail/Blocked with exact stage, confirmed/suspected cause, next trigger and unaffected work.
9. Replay from saved data after mapper/template corrections rather than recollect by default.

Use route-specific actual scope. Meta parent ads differ from cards/advertisers; marketplace search order differs from sales rank; Trends relative indices/bounds differ from absolute demand; QC depends on location/pack/time; supplier terms depend on company/product/workflow.

## 5. Limits and legacy evidence

Existing paid trial ceilings/scopes remain in active configuration/journal until their explicitly planned migration: original total US$0.75 and US$3.75 future allocation are not replenished by a new test, key or date. Three approved accounts add only actually verified funding; original recovery stays origin-bound. See actual existing [runner commands](offline_runner_guide.md) rather than assuming the future router is already available.

Public/direct trials incur no Apify launch fee on that route, but preserve all effort and provider restrictions. No paid search/proxy/commerce pack, supplier membership or new cash purchase automatically.

AI starts with a small useful group and extends finite recorded groups within the chosen signed-in GPT-6 Luna/High subscription route. The current runner allows at most256KiB of public input,10 items per request and180 seconds; named practical fit reviews group at most5 compatible items and the default pass selects at most10 named questions. The separate90-second feasibility probe is not the normal runner deadline. Owned-process receipts and exact original job/runner replay apply. Usage thresholds resize later work; they are not quality gates. Preserve completed output/unknown usage; no blind retry of potentially running jobs or new paid route.

## 6. Supplier and selection samples

Start a small supplier experiment with a few actual coherent products,1–2 grounded queries and initially up to3 relevant pages each; terms/FAQ page when readily useful. New versioned search plans can explicitly allow up to5. Keep old three-lead replay and actual no-result/blocked outcomes.

Exercise exact/close/family lead, stated no subscription/required/optional/unknown, monthly/annual/setup/deposit/per-order fees, single-piece purchase/dispatch/bulkstockthenindividual, COD/integration/neutralpack/private label and quoted pack/MOQ. MOQ 1 alone does not prove dropshipping. Supplier relevance and stated terms are not verification. No supplier contact/purchase.

Selection cases cover broad categories/moderate brands, the exact ten active original IDs, Good>=6/Decent=5 both included, retired rows excluded, legacy13 compatibility and unknown versus supported negative, two assessable core families/onepositive, useful inference, all-history identity/novelty and queued holds. Zero substantiated supplier leads after a completed cursory search remains acceptable.

## 7. Repeat supply and capacity measurement

Measure current checkpoint separately from a later collection. Freeze prior concept/query/history baseline; choose next questions using actual feedback; report new-to-research, carryover, new-to-client, duplicate-heavy/unrelated output and semantic uncertainty. A same-day pair proves rotation/replay, not recurring market replenishment.

At the legacy seven-of-thirteen checkpoint 11/156=7.05% ready with 38 unfinished; this is not measured ten-criterion yield. Future completed-screening yield has its own denominator. Use 5% planning/3% stress scenarios without promising either floor. Include failed/rejected research, read holds, update validation, supplier/model effort, new-source mix and inventory depletion in costs.

A representative approved weekly rehearsal aims at three distinct minimum-ten issues, minimums before extras and>=80% New each. First complete approved issue can proceed independently; shortfall elsewhere stays visible. Filling one issue continues only the remaining finite research allowance.

Generate actual Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday 09:00 India dates, including 12–14 monthly releases and asynchronous account periods. Check source expiry at intended availability; retained archives are historical, not refreshed daily. No multiplication of one weekly capture solely because it supports three files.

Test first-two bilingual ordinals/cancellation; third language-pending facts and workbook-only finishing path. Use isolated simulated history for tests, never manufacture live publication history.

## 8. Findings and decision rule

Each experiment/rehearsal reports actual attempts/results/yield/cost/time, concrete defects, known uncertainty, independent work available and next scoped action. If volume and approximate funded headroom conflict, discuss actual figures and options; no weak padding, automatic short publication, relaxed novelty/checklist, paid upgrade or invented supplier/market facts.

Store detailed evidence in existing trial/research/job/provider directories. Update concise results in the benchmark record and current proof status in the master plan. Do not create narrative documents per correction. A successful trial is evidence for its route only; a full service still needs its explicit integration and real-output acceptance.
