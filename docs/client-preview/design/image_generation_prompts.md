# P5 Product Visual Prompt Record

**Generation method:** OpenAI built-in image generation  
**Use case:** `product-mockup`  
**Created for:** NeuraFlow Product Opportunity Radar manufactured preview  
**Date:** 15 August 2026

---

## Shared art direction and constraints

Every asset used this locked direction:

- premium editorial still-life for a portrait research report;
- photorealistic but understated rather than commercial;
- matte, slightly pastel surfaces;
- warm cream plaster, pale stone, and a restrained muted accent;
- soft diffused daylight and natural contact shadows;
- portrait-friendly 4:5 composition with generous breathing room;
- correct, clearly recognisable generic product identity;
- no seller listing, packaging, brand, logo, readable text, watermark, price, badge, person, endorsement, certification, exaggerated effect, or performance claim.

The prompts below are the final subject-specific requests used with that shared direction.

---

## 01 — Oil Spray Bottle

Create one unbranded clear glass cooking-oil mister with a matte ivory spray top, empty of labels or readable markings, on a warm cream plaster backdrop and pale stone surface. Keep the full bottle visible and recognisably designed for cooking oil.

## 02 — Rechargeable Cordless Picture Light

Create one unbranded slim horizontal picture light with a narrow warm-brass bar, compact wall-mount plate, cordless construction, no visible cable, and no readable controls. Place it on a cream plaster wall with only a plain frame edge for scale.

## 03 — Motion-Activated Cat Ball

Create one unbranded compact spherical interactive cat toy with a matte ivory and muted-sage shell, subtle rolling seams, and a recessed charging port. Keep the ball large and clear at a low three-quarter angle; do not show an animal or motion trail.

## 04 — Silicone Suction Feeding Set

Create one coordinated unbranded infant-feeding set containing a divided suction plate, suction bowl, small open cup, soft bib, silicone spoon, and fork. Arrange the dusty-rose and oatmeal pieces neatly as one product set with no food or person.

## 05 — Manual Vegetable Chopper and Slicer

Create one unbranded transparent chopper bowl with a matte ivory manual push lid, visible removable metal blade assembly, and a simple slicing attachment beside it. Make its non-electric operation visually clear; show no food.

## 06 — Pleated Mushroom Table Lamp

Create one unbranded compact table lamp with a rounded mushroom silhouette, softly pleated shade, simple matte base, and gentle warm diffused light. Place it on a pale stone side table in a calm cream interior.

## 07 — Self-Watering Plant Spike Set

Create one coordinated unbranded set of tapered plant-watering spikes with adjustable drip caps and slim feed tubes. Arrange the pieces clearly beside one small potted plant without an exaggerated water stream or growth claim.

## 08 — Self-Cleaning Pet Grooming Brush

Create one unbranded ergonomic handheld grooming brush with a matte ivory handle, muted navy-grey pin bed, fine rounded metal pins, and one visible release button. Show no pet, person, loose fur, or coat-health claim.

## 09 — Stroller Organiser Bag

Create one unbranded structured organiser bag in muted navy-grey canvas with two adjustable attachment straps, an open central compartment, two bottle sleeves, and a small front pocket. Use one plain curved rail only to explain the straps; show no stroller brand, baby, or contents.

## 10 — Adjustable Baby-Proof Cabinet Lock Set

Create one coordinated unbranded set of flexible white cabinet-safety strap locks with round adhesive anchor pads and adjustable clasps. Lay several pieces on pale stone and position one across a plain cream cabinet edge, without a child-safety guarantee.

## 11 — Clip-On Sink Strainer Basket

Create one unbranded small perforated oval drainage basket in muted sage, clipped visibly to the inside edge of a plain matte stainless-steel sink. Keep the basket empty and show no food, water splash, dishes, or fit claim.

## 12 — Silicone Paw Cleaner Cup

Create one unbranded cylindrical paw-cleaning cup in muted dusty rose with a removable lid beside it and soft internal silicone bristles visible from a slightly elevated angle. Show no animal, paw, water, dirt, or hygiene claim.

## Cover — Product Research Still Life

Create a quiet editorial still-life representing broad product research across home, kitchen, pet, and baby categories. Arrange a clear cooking-oil mister, small pleated mushroom lamp, muted navy grooming brush, and dusty-rose silicone feeding bowl on pale stone plinths. Keep the grouping sculptural, calm, unbranded, and secondary to generous cover-copy space.
