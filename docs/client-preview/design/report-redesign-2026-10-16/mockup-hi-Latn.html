Silicone suction feeding set

Jaldi ka daavAchha fit

Dilchaspi nayi hai aur badh rahi hai, par abhi saabit nahi.

Yeh product kyun

Silicone ka feeding set jiska suction base table ko pakad leta hai, taaki chhote bachche bowl ko aasani se door na dhakel sakein. Dilchaspi nayi hai, abhi saabit nahi: India mein Google search tezi se badhi hai aur BaapStore ne ise abhi new arrival mein joda hai, lekin abhi kuch hi advertisers Meta par iske ads chala rahe hain.

  1. India mein iski search haal ke hafton mein tezi se badhi hai.
  2. BaapStore ise apni new-arrivals list mein dikha raha hai.
  3. Amazon aur Flipkart par milti-julti listings madhyam sankhya mein hain, isliye kuch jagah bachi hai.

Dhyan rakhein

Abhi advertisers kam hain, isliye maang shuruaati hai aur saabit nahi hui. Baby ke khane wale items ke material aur safety daavon ko listing se pehle client khud check kare.
