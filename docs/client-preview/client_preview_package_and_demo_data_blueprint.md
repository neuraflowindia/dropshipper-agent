## 0. Purpose and Authority

This document defines the package that will be shown to a prospective client before any application is built.

It answers five practical questions:

1. What exactly will the client receive?
2. What does the sample research contain?
3. How will the Excel workbook be organised?
4. How will the full portrait PDF be organised?
5. What commercial promise can be made safely at the proposed founding price?

The product brief remains the authority for the eventual system, signal logic, provider rules, security, and implementation. This blueprint is the authority for the manufactured preview data and the client-facing sample outputs.

If the two documents conflict:

- use the product brief for how the eventual service must operate;
- use this blueprint for the pre-sales package and its synthetic sample data;
- correct the documents before creating either sample artifact.

---

## 1. Decision Summary

The next deliverable is not an application. It is a convincing but honest preview package.

The approved production order is:

1. freeze this blueprint and its manufactured dataset;
2. create the sample Excel workbook from the dataset;
3. inspect and correct the workbook;
4. create the full portrait PDF from the same dataset;
5. inspect and correct the PDF;
6. write the brochure from the proven outputs;
7. show the package to the prospective client;
8. build the application only after commercial interest or commitment.

This order prevents the brochure from promising features that the actual outputs do not demonstrate.

---

## 2. Preview Objective

The preview must make the service easy to understand in less than five minutes while still giving the client enough detail to inspect the evidence.

It must demonstrate:

- a ranked weekly shortlist;
- evidence from three core signal families;
- modest category balance;
- week-over-week movement;
- contextual quick-commerce observations;
- lightweight safety and compliance flags;
- supplier leads with visible evidence limits;
- a mobile-friendly portrait report;
- a detailed but usable Excel evidence pack;
- a repeatable subscription service rather than a one-time research exercise.

The preview must not imply that manufactured examples are live findings.

---

## 3. Explicit Non-Objectives

The pre-sales preview phase does not include:

- application development;
- a client portal or dashboard;
- live provider calls;
- paid provider activity;
- credentials or secret keys;
- automation or scheduling;
- real market claims;
- real supplier verification;
- automated ordering;
- sales or profit forecasting;
- legal or regulatory certification;
- a public website;
- a final long-term pricing structure.

P6 adds a brochure and operator demo kit to the already approved Excel and PDF. It does not change any of the remaining exclusions above.

No action in this phase starts the seven-day application implementation window.

## 4. Demo Disclosure and Evidence Boundary

The client understands that the preview uses made-up demonstration data. Keep that disclosure discreet rather than repeating it throughout the package.

Use this single line:

> Illustrative preview using sample data; not a live market-research report.

Placement:

- once on the PDF cover only;
- once near the top of the Excel Shortlist sheet only;
- once in the eventual brochure when the sample output is shown;
- nowhere else unless the artifacts are materially separated or repurposed for another audience.

Additional rules:

- no real person or business is presented as a supplier;
- supplier names are plausible but fictional and are governed by the artifact-level sample-data disclosure;
- all external links use reserved example.com addresses;
- all dates, rankings, prices, counts, and trend values are manufactured;
- images must be generated, licensed, or clearly marked as illustrative;
- labels such as Strong or Promising describe the sample scoring logic, not real demand;
- no language may claim a product is guaranteed to sell, compliant, safe, profitable, or verified.

## 5. Commercial Hypothesis

### 5.1 Founding-client offer

| Item | Founding offer |
|---|---:|
| Client price | ₹1,500 per month |
| Billing | Monthly, paid in advance |
| Delivery frequency | Four weekly packages per month |

| Custom research requests | Not included |
| Client portal | Not included |
| Supplier verification | Not included |

This is a pilot price designed to secure and learn from the first client. It is not yet a proven long-term price.

The offer is month-to-month and paid in advance. One billing cycle contains four deliveries spaced approximately one week apart from the agreed start date. The ₹1,500 founding price is held for the first two paid cycles and is reviewed only before a later renewal; it is never changed mid-cycle.

### 5.2 Internal cash-cost envelope

| Cost area | Expected monthly cash cost | Control |
|---|---:|---|
| Apify | ₹0 while usage remains inside the included US$5 allowance | Alert before 85% of the allowance |
| QuickCommerceAPI | About ₹500 | Do not exceed the planned monthly credit pack |
| AI analysis | About ₹25–₹100 | Use a low-cost model and strict input/output limits |
| PDF and Excel generation | ₹0 locally | No paid rendering dependency in V1 |
| Storage and delivery | ₹0 in the operator-run V1 | Local files and manual delivery |
| Expected total | About ₹525–₹600 | Plan conservatively at ₹600–₹750 |

These are working assumptions, not a guarantee. Provider pricing and actual usage must be rechecked before application development.

### 5.3 Commercial control points

| Level | Amount or condition | Required response |
|---|---:|---|
| Healthy target | At or below ₹750 per month | Continue and measure |
| Review trigger | Above ₹1,000 per month | Investigate and consciously accept the lower margin, simplify, or reprice |
| Permitted pilot maximum | Up to ₹1,500 per month | Allowed for a deliberately subsidised pilot; commercially unsustainable as a routine state |
| Above client charge | Above ₹1,500 per month | Reduce scope, reprice, or discontinue before continuing |
| Time warning | More than 2 operator hours per month after stabilisation | Simplify the workflow or reprice |
| Time failure | More than 4 operator hours per month | The ₹1,500 offer is not commercially sustainable |

At ₹600 direct cost, ₹900 remains before paying for operator time, tax, support, or business overhead. At ₹1,000 direct cost, ₹500 remains. At ₹1,500 direct cost, the cash margin is zero before operator time and overhead. The pilot may consciously absorb that cost, but it must not be mistaken for a sustainable operating model.

### 5.4 What the client price buys

The ₹1,500 monthly offer includes:

- four scheduled weekly research packages per monthly billing cycle;
- up to 12 eligible opportunities in each report;
- a portrait PDF decision brief;
- a three-sheet Excel evidence workbook;
- week-over-week labels after a comparable prior run exists;
- supplier leads for selected products, subject to evidence availability;
- lightweight compliance-review flags;
- manual delivery by the operator.

It does not include:

- unlimited requests;
- a guaranteed count of 12 products;
- guaranteed supplier leads;
- supplier negotiation or verification;
- product compliance decisions;
- store integration;
- advertisements, creative production, or media buying;
- sales, margin, or inventory forecasts;
- a software login;
- real-time or daily monitoring.

### 6.4 Signal rules used in the preview

The sample must apply the product brief rules exactly:

- **Meta positive:** at least 3 relevant advertisers, or at least 5 active relevant ads, and the oldest active relevant ad is at least 14 days old.
- **Marketplace positive:** Amazon Bestseller evidence, Amazon Movers & Shakers evidence, or relevant results on both Amazon and Flipkart with at least one top-10 result.
- **Search positive:** a consistent 12-month India series where the recent four-week average is at least 1.15 times the prior four-week average and increases by at least 5 points, or a supported related rising query.
- **Strong:** 3 positive core signals.
- **Promising:** 2 positive core signals.
- **Worth Watching:** 1 positive core signal.
- **Weak:** no positive core signals.

Missing evidence is not negative evidence. Each row therefore records both positive-signal count and available-signal count.

### 6.5 Weekly-status rules used in the preview

- **New:** selected this week and absent from the prior comparable shortlist.
- **Returning:** selected in both weeks with the same label and comparable coverage.
- **Strengthened:** selected in both weeks, coverage is comparable, and the label improved.
- **Weakened:** selected in both weeks, coverage is comparable, and the label declined.
- **Coverage Changed:** selected in both weeks but the available evidence families changed, so directional comparison would be misleading.

The first real report delivered to a client must label every selected product New because no prior comparable run exists.

## 10. Illustrative Supplier-Lead Dataset

### 10.1 Lead rules

The sample contains three supplier leads per product so the workbook and PDF can demonstrate useful layouts. This does not create a real-service guarantee.

Every lead must:

- use a plausible but fictional business name;
- use an example.com address;
- use a realistic URL status such as Reachable, Redirected, Unchecked, Blocked, or Unreachable;
- preserve missing price, MOQ, or evidence as missing;
- avoid the words verified, approved, trusted, or recommended;
- state what was actually visible in the manufactured source record;
- remain separate from opportunity scoring.
