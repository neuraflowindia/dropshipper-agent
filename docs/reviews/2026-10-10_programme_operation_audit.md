## 1. What we are trying to finish

The goal is a program that keeps finding useful, narrowly defined kinds of products in four agreed categories, checks them sensibly, finds possible suppliers, and prepares three different private reports each week. The reports are intended for Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 9:00 AM India time. Each should normally contain 10–15 entries, with a minimum of ten and up to twenty when worthwhile. The first two reports need English and Roman Hinglish versions. The owner approves client release separately.

The program must remember earlier work, control paid requests, keep useful work moving when an individual attempt fails, and explain what happened without requiring someone to reconstruct the story from hundreds of files. Functional photos, source figures, checklist reasons, supplier information and working downloads are included. Visual redesign is outside the present scope.

## 5. Audit conclusion

The application is not missing all records. It already retains original research, AI requests/answers, source attempt identities, financial history and report versions. The missing connection is between those detailed records and a timely, understandable view of what the program is doing and whether it is making useful progress.

There are seven connected repair findings in implementation section 28. Fixing them requires small changes to the current program, not another database, paid monitoring product, scheduler or background service. No AI is needed to record errors, measure time, enforce allowances or explain a known saved status. AI remains appropriate for product judgments and genuinely necessary answer correction.

The next implementation should first repair progress and recording together; then resolve the nine answers and finish current research; then produce and privately verify the two updated report sets; then complete backup/restart/next-round proof. An isolated failure must record its reason and next action and yield to other eligible work. If everything is genuinely waiting, the program must save its position and return until the next real trigger, not spin or invent successful results.
