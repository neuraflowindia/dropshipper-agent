## Complexity and continuation decision

The new operation recording is justified by the owner's need to understand what is running and why a step failed. It uses Python's standard logging and one small advisory file rather than adding another server, database, queue or monitoring programme. Its complexity-to-current-requirement assessment is 2 out of 10 for this recording addition, a reviewer judgment rather than a measured project score. Maintenance hours cannot be estimated from this focused review and are not invented.

Keep the existing budget, original-receipt and single-writer controls. Do not add automatic termination merely because the advisory five-minute preparation warning appears; it is not proof of an unsafe or dead process. Decline further recording infrastructure unless it solves a demonstrated missing record or current fault. If diagnostics interfere with research or expose private content, repair or disable that diagnostic path while preserving the original research records and safe independent work. No new numeric spending thresholds or automatic shutdown rules were introduced by this review.

The next connected acceptance remains the original programme's actual answers and relevant source/supplier/photo outcomes, two updated bilingual private report sets and unchanged private downloads, plus the existing backup/cost/restart checks. Local and hosted release gates remain excluded under the owner's newest instruction.
