## 1. Purpose and document ownership

NeuraFlow provides India-focused product research and possible supplier leads for one dropshipping/private-label client. The owner operates and reviews the service; the client reads and downloads approved reports through a private portal. Recommendations are opportunities worth testing, not sales, profit or RTO predictions.

Operate on a best-effort basis with normal research variation and reasonable labelled judgments. Approximate size/weight/handling inferred from actual form/mechanism is acceptable; do not demand measured packed weight or exact specifications for an ordinary sensible fit judgment. Label estimates, distinguish them from measurements and do not infer margin/RTO. Keep explicit delivery requirements, accurate source/accounting records and private access; do not require owner approval for routine searches, interpretation or verified intermediate milestones.

This brief owns product scope, selection, evidence and output requirements. The [cost plan](../operations/build_activation_and_costs_2026-10-03.md) owns budgets/forecast arithmetic; the [execution specification](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md) owns mechanisms/acceptance criteria; [Progress](../project/pj-tracker.md) owns current dated status; the [benchmark record](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/benchmark_record.md) retains historical measurements. ## 3. Product identity, brand judgment and continuity

Recommend a narrowly defined functional **type**, not one manufacturer's SKU and not an overly broad category. For example, “Adjustable doorway pull-up bar” is the opportunity; actual matching sellers/products/supplier offers sit underneath it. Preserve meaningful mechanism/mounting/use/age constraints. Ordinary colour, pack, seller wording or decorative finish is not a new recommendation. Multiple actual examples are desirable when useful, but two examples/two suppliers are not new eligibility gates. One substantiated example can be included honestly. Do not create a fictitious combined product by joining features from unrelated listings.

The existing concept identity already intends that functional meaning. Strengthen it with a versioned definition, manufacturer-independent report title and actual example/offer bindings, without replacing stable IDs or rewriting raw descriptions/history. Catalogue families remain research headings, not automatically recommendation identities. A broad heading may lead to several distinct narrow types. The implementation and historical merge/split/alias handling are in [section26.3](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#263-one-identity-explicit-meaning-and-actual-examples).

Assign one primary category and optional secondary tags. Home/Decor and Home/Kitchen overlap must not create duplicate products. Preserve canonical concept ID, aliases, product features and original source names.

Brand judgment is moderate, not a strict gate:
- Exclude recognisable established brand-specific products, such as Lakme, Hasbro or Meyer products.
- A named retailer, manufacturer name or unfamiliar small label does not itself disqualify a product.
- Accept reasonable generic/private-label candidates without demanding confirmed relabelling permission.
- Retain the judgment and reason, including borderline cases. A potential private-label candidate is not a confirmed supplier agreement.

The current AI description step supplies generic/small, established or unclear; code follows that judgment and does not automatically exclude an unfamiliar/unclear name. This is not a hard brand dictionary. Product versus generic service is a separate interpretation, and supplier e-commerce services are separate term fields. P2's explicit source-bound correction can restore a mistaken brand-only early exclusion before identity/scoring, including carrying its valid verdict into a new concept without overwriting an existing matched concept's judgment. Original answers/history remain; no routine second AI check is added. Automatic named brand-model dispatch remains later work in plan section21.18.

Exact duplicates merge; near matches use feature/function comparison. Different sizes/colours/sellers do not create new recommendations; meaningful functional, child/adult or powered/manual differences may. Ordinary supported AI judgments can be accepted after reference/feature checks. Only material unresolved ambiguity or published-history conflicts need targeted review; no manual approval of every semantic link. New status is computed from history after the identity decision, not guessed by AI.

“New” means never previously published to this client, not absent from only the last issue and not newly invented. Use all published history plus product allocations in already-approved upcoming issues. Allocate a concept to only one issue in a weekly batch by default. An update needs a material supported change and a short “what changed” reason; identical repeated evidence is not an update.

For actual issue size N: updates <= floor(N / 5), new >= N - floor(N / 5).
Examples: 10 => at least 8 new/at most 2 updates; 12 => 10/2; 15 => 12/3. No obligation to include updates. At ten per issue, three issues require 30 entries and at least 24 new products; with no eligible updates, all 30 must be new.

Maintain separate first-seen, first-published, last-observed and last-published dates. Drafts, benchmark products and manufactured preview data are not published history. Track queued allocations separately so preparing three issues together cannot double-count a product as new. If an earlier queued issue is cancelled, reconcile later allocations before publication.

## 4. Ten-criterion product fit

The active checklist has ten criteria. Retire original IDs **3 (High margin), 4 (Past winners) and 10 (Extremely unique)** from current scoring, reasons, selection and new client reports. Keep their historical records unchanged. Preserve the original IDs of the ten remaining criteria; do not renumber them or award points for retired criteria.

| Active score | Fit tier and report treatment |
|---|---|
| 6–10 Meets | **Good fit**; combined source coverage determines its priority tier. |
| 5 Meets | **Decent fit**; eligible under the same combined source-priority policy. |
| 0–4 Meets, enough unknowns to potentially reach five | **More fit information needed**; not a proved product failure. Use a bounded practical judgment/review if useful. |
| 0–4 Meets, even all unknowns cannot reach five | Below the agreed fit threshold on current judgments. Keep the reasons; reopen only for a relevant change or named review. |

There is no minimum quota of Good-fit products. A useful ten-product issue can contain both tiers, or Decent-fit products alone when they satisfy the combined source priorities below, identity, supplier-work and novelty requirements. Fit is suitability, not market performance or a promise of sales. Five Matches with two usable related market sites ranks before six Matches with only one reasonably positive site.

| Original ID / active criterion | Practical assessment guidance |
|---|---|
| 1. Small in size | Use known dimensions or described/visible form. An explained inference is sufficient; missing exact dimensions are not an automatic negative. |
| 2. Easy to ship | Consider likely handling, size, form, approximate weight and fragility. Reasonable labelled estimation is sufficient; no exact packed weight or shipping quote is required, and charges are not invented. |
| 5. Improves confidence | Identify a plausible product-specific confidence benefit, with its inferred basis. |
| 6. Improves convenience | Name the task made easier. Convenience need not also save time. |
| 7. Saves people time | Use a practical AI judgment about steps, handling or repeated work. A draining bowl may avoid transferring food into a separate sieve. Label the mechanism as inferred when not measured; do not invent minutes or a percentage. |
| 8. Solves a problem/fills a gap | Identify the actual problem/use case and how the product addresses it. |
| 9. Saves people money | Explain a plausible avoided expense or supported comparison. This concerns the customer, not seller margin. |
| 11. Improves quality of life | State a concrete everyday benefit without generic praise or medical promises. |
| 12. High perceived value | Assess usefulness, presentation or practical value; no profit estimate or exact margin is required. |
| 13. Women-centric audience | Use a moderate Indian-consumer positioning judgment, not a demand for measured gender percentages. Store women-centric, men-centric, unisex or unclear with a short reason. |

Each active criterion has **Meets, Does not meet, Unclear or Not assessed**, a reason, supplied source references where applicable, and observed/inferred/not-stated/conflicting basis. Code counts only supported Meets. Unclear and Not assessed contribute no point but are not negative evidence. Does not meet needs a supported practical reason; absence of proof alone is Unclear.

Audience describes likely buyers/users and product positioning, not what every man or woman does. A plain suitcase is ordinarily unisex; a beard-specific razor is ordinarily men-centric. An ordinary household kitchen aid may plausibly be women-centric in the client's Indian market without demographic statistics. Do not automatically mark every kitchen item women-centric, transfer a male positioning assumption to every nutrition product, or confuse a baby's functional user with the purchaser. Reasonable product-specific inference can earn the women-centric point; supported men-centric/unisex positioning normally does not earn that point, but **does not exclude the product**. Unclear remains unknown. Buyer positioning stays separate from functional identity.

For time-saving, ask the AI to name the ordinary task, likely changed step and plausible saving mechanism, or explain no plausible saving/unknown. Do not add a stopwatch, measured-data requirement or another model call per criterion. A “ten units before, fewer after” example is optional explanatory illustration only, clearly labelled hypothetical; it is never a measured value, market signal or basis for arithmetic points. The normal report uses a short phrase such as “Fewer transfers while draining.”

Overlapping benefits can count separately when each has a sensible product-specific explanation. Do not require documentary proof for obvious practical benefits or add a confidence-percentage gate. A named review can raise or lower judgments, keeps the original result, and is reused for unchanged inputs rather than repeated until the score passes.

Implementation contracts: [ten-criterion migration](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#2116-ten-criterion-fit-policy-and-coordinated-migration), [source priorities](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#24-n03-current-source-support-and-n25-record-recovery-contracts), and [practical answer ownership](../operations/implementation_2026-10-11.md#r03-practical-judgments-and-exact-answer-ownership). ## 6. Market assessment and selection

Keep market evidence separate from the ten-criterion fit score and Good/Decent tier. Current selection distinguishes seven sites: Meta, Amazon, Flipkart, Google Trends, Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart. Amazon's three routes count as one site. Relevant reviewed quick-commerce results can provide neutral site support, not positive demand inferred from stock or price. Supplier searches never add market votes. The earlier three-family grouping remains only for the legacy numerical diagnostics below; it is not a current two-source exclusion gate.

Existing numerical measurements, retained for context rather than universal exclusion:
- Meta positive: at least three distinct relevant advertisers OR at least five relevant active ads with oldest relevant active ad at least 14 days old.
- Marketplace positive: relevant Amazon Bestseller/Movers & Shakers presence OR relevant matches on Amazon and Flipkart with at least one in its first ten returned positions. Returned search order is not sales rank.
- Trends: consistent India 12-month series; exclude incomplete periods; at least four nonzero points. Compare last four comparable periods with preceding four; rising requires ratio >= 1.15 and increase >= five points. Supported same-product rising related queries may satisfy the alternative rule. With zero previous average, require at least two recent nonzero periods plus a supporting rising query; otherwise insufficient/stable as appropriate. Missing/sparse/all-zero data is insufficient, not negative demand.

States per family: Positive (available true, positive true), Not-positive (true, false), Insufficient or Unavailable (false, null), with method/reason and source references. A positive OR branch suffices; absence of a required field cannot establish failure of every branch. Store exact available families, not just counts.

The old Strong/Promising/Worth-Watching/Weak labels describe whether numerical thresholds were met; they do not establish commercial success or failure. Current priority distinguishes usable relevant coverage from practical support. Flat interest is not automatically negative and does not fail a two-source product. For one-source products, a bounded product-specific interpretation may find reasonable support in steady interest or useful marketplace/ad context without demanding the old growth thresholds.

Selection order:
1. Check identity/category, moderate brand decision and lightweight safety flags.
2. Require five supported Matches across the ten active criteria using practical observed/inferred reasons. Unknowns are not failures; complete their actual assessment rather than presume negative results.
3. Prioritise in this exact order: at least six Matches with at least two usable related market sites; five with at least two; at least six with one reasonably positive site; five with one reasonably positive site. Two-site tiers have no additional positive-growth gate. One-site tiers disclose narrower support and need a real favourable interpretation, not empty/unrelated data. Count Meta, Amazon, Flipkart, Google Trends, Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart separately when their actual related result is usable; all Amazon lists/searches count as one site. Suppliers are not market votes. Quick-commerce stock alone is neutral context, not sales proof or automatic positive support.
4. Within a priority tier, use meaningful support, score, useful category balance/freshness and stable concept ID. Preserve required New share and holds; never insist on ten Good-fit products or exclude solely because a numerical growth threshold was missed.
5. Record actual discouraging information and obvious unsuitable products with a specific reason. Category balance does not justify weak padding, and an old label alone is not a completed suitability judgment.
6. Allocate products to issues, enforce each issue's size and novelty, perform supplier search and validate the package.
7. If insufficient eligible products remain, save exact shortfall/rejection counts and ask the owner. Continue independent work; do not auto-publish a short report.

Movement comparisons require comparable source-family sets and collection/query/assessment policies. A changed policy/coverage is not strengthened demand. Reusing the same observation across issue dates is not a new trend measurement.

## 7. Supplier search and recurring-fee preference

Cursory discovery is required for every recommended product. Aim for 1–3 useful leads, up to five when easily available; zero substantiated leads is acceptable after actual recorded effort. This includes genuinely attempted searches/routes that fail or produce no usable pages/interpretation. An otherwise eligible product can appear with **“Supplier search did not produce any usable results.”** Keep exact empty/blocked/error/pending details internally; do not claim the product/supply is unavailable or no suppliers exist. Preserve useful subsequent follow-up without holding the product indefinitely. Prepared links or prelaunch-only checks are not actual supplier effort.

Search direct Indian manufacturers/wholesalers/importers, IndiaMART, TradeIndia and Google Search/Maps. Owner reports permission for the proposed Maps supplier/manufacturer discovery. Test browser automation within that scope: background/headless first, visible browser if needed. Do not bypass CAPTCHA/access controls; record blocks and use a permitted alternative or guided manual step. Do not contact suppliers, request quotes, buy access or create accounts automatically.

For each lead record:
- Candidate/concept ID; supplier name, type, location when supported.
- Product/company page URL, retrieval status, timestamp and evidence excerpt/reference.
- Match type: exact/close product or broader relevant manufacturer/company lead.
- Supply arrangement: single-piece purchase with direct customer dispatch; bulk stock purchase followed by individual dispatch; bulk supply only; or Not stated. Record the evidence for each part separately; “MOQ 1” alone does not establish dropshipping.
- Minimum purchase quantity and any stock prepayment/deposit, where stated. Hundreds of separate single-item customer orders are not one bulk purchase.
- E-commerce support as separate stated/unknown fields: direct dispatch, COD, store/order integration, neutral packaging and private-label services. “E-commerce support” alone establishes none of these.
- Private-label support: stated yes, stated no, or unknown; not mandatory for retaining a plausible lead.
- Subscription status: No subscription required — stated; Subscription required; Optional paid plan; Not stated.
- Mandatory fee amount/frequency where stated; separate setup fee, deposit, MOQ and per-order charge fields.
- Short suitability/limitation note.

Check readily accessible pricing/FAQ/reseller/dropshipping pages. No pricing page means Not stated, not no fee. Free signup does not prove free fulfilment access. Consider recurring annual plans too, not only monthly subscriptions; optional paid tiers must be genuinely optional for the proposed workflow.

Among relevant leads, prefer a usable no-subscription route. Unknown terms do not exclude a plausible supplier; mandatory-subscription suppliers remain lower-priority alternatives. Product relevance and fulfilment usefulness still matter. Do not rank an irrelevant “free” supplier first. Reuse dated supplier-level terms across related leads; distinguish platform membership, buyer access and seller advertising charges.

Supplier discovery must progress beyond basic first-hit links: seek useful Indian product/company leads, read readily available terms, and record the real queries/fetched or blocked pages. An empty completed search is valid; actually attempted searches with no usable result are also sufficient best-effort work for inclusion with the limitation. These are distinct internal outcomes, not a failed search relabelled successful. A prepared query/manual link alone is not an actual attempt. No minimum number of successful suppliers is imposed. Known company-wide terms may be reused up to30 days at their actual scope; product price/MOQ/stock conditions are not automatically company-wide. Client PDF/workbook/summary use plain wording and the corresponding natural Roman-Hinglish meaning, not raw internal failure codes.

A source page is evidence of stated terms, not verification of capability or permission. Google Maps business presence alone does not prove a product match or all-India dropshipping. Label all results supplier leads. Search-result links may support a clearly labelled manual follow-up route, not invented factual rows.

- Protect every report/download route, not only the homepage. No public storage listing or alternate unauthenticated object URL.
- Uploaded drafts remain private; only approved, valid packages with release time reached are visible.
- Client never triggers research or controls provider credentials. Daily access does not imply daily new reports.
- No public marketing site, billing integration, chat assistant or multi-client administration in this build.

Keep all encountered records within explicit capture limits, including irrelevant/rejected rows and duplicate mappings. Record pagination/cap truncation so “all encountered” never implies complete-market coverage. Distinguish excluded, deferred for later, duplicate, blocked source and not yet assessed. Record the primary reason plus other material reasons, not a vague “failed.”

Internal funnel: captured records -> described items -> distinct concepts -> completed screening -> eligible -> allocated -> published. Separate currently rejected/set-aside from unfinished. Saved legacy-policy 11/156 readiness is not a new ten-criterion yield or a final pass rate; cost forecasting includes low-yield scenarios, repeats, failures and backlog depletion rather than counting only successful recommendations. Log per-category and per-reason counts to diagnose over-filtering. Owner corrections preserve original decisions and append rationale; client PDF stays concise.

Enough products for one issue ends its selection, not all research. Continue the explicitly remaining finite run allowance on useful saved interpretation, missing evidence, supplier work and later-report inventory. Do not create an unlimited extra allowance or re-investigate every weak product. Use a small scheduled continuation and saved state, not a dependency on the next chat message.

Every action needs a failure route: preserve valid siblings/artifacts, identify the affected item/source/account/language/issue, distinguish observed failure from suspected cause, choose an implemented permitted alternative or other dependency-ready work, and save an exact reopening trigger. Shared outages produce grouped diagnostics, not repeated per-product calls. Unknown possibly charged/running work is reconciled at its origin; it is not relaunchable merely to keep moving. A canonical-write failure prevents new dependent spending, while existing online releases stay intact. Internal facts/workbooks/finished editions may survive an incomplete report; they cannot be mislabelled complete or published. The detailed action/paired-failure contract is owned by plan section 21.17.

Publication is a release of an approved immutable package, not a research run. Stage all artifacts, validate hashes/content, then commit the approved manifest last. Gate discovery and downloads on approval and release time using server time. Repeated requests/releases cannot create duplicate history; published means available to the client, not necessarily read. Future approved issues reserve concept allocations but do not become published history early. Failures leave prior releases intact; affected incomplete/short issues remain unavailable with an operator alert.

A correction is a new revision of the same issue, not a new product discovery or extra scheduled issue. Preserve prior revision provenance; revoke withdrawn packages from normal portal access. Do not promise to revoke files already downloaded by a client.

## 11. Storage, reuse and security

Use local structured files for research/audit and private hosted package storage for the portal; no paid database required merely for one client. Store raw/cache/state under `data/`; frozen live facts/workbooks/manifests under `data/outputs/<issue>/revision-<n>/`; live PDFs/HTML/review images under `media/live/reports/<issue>/revision-<n>/`; public config under `config/`; secrets only in ignored local storage.

Physical retrieval keys include actual source/query/location/filter/period/node/depth and options affecting requested material. Parser/schema/interpretation versions bind original capture hashes separately; mapping/format fixes replay saved bytes without recollection. AI keys also include relevant meaning/evidence/model/prompt/assessment version, while presentation-only changes do not reset identity. Weekly captures can serve scheduled issues for up to seven days from observation; this is a freshness default, not live-data proof or a ban on useful independent free exploration. Keep original dates. Company-wide terms may be reused up to30 days at their actual scope unless contradicted; Trends up to seven days. Review stale/conflicting evidence before selection, not blindly refresh beyond budget.

Unknown fields/charges stay unknown, never zero. Use decimal money; persist reservations before charged requests and retain unknown-cost holds. Origin account and provider period own money, not token or environment labels. Routing new work to another approved funded account does not move or reset prior jobs/charges. Prevent overlapping charged work, reconcile uncertain submissions before retrying, and bound all network/process work. Paid GETs are not safe automatic retries.

Treat external content/model responses as untrusted: validate source IDs and complete structured output; escape HTML and spreadsheet formula prefixes; block unsafe URL schemes/private destinations/redirects; bound decoded image/body sizes. Source text cannot issue commands, change budgets or access secrets. Never publish keys, auth files, private account identifiers, raw diagnostics or internal usage.

Back up essential history, checked data, packages and nonsecret application files inside the owner-selected project `data/backups`, and prove exact-copy/isolated-restore behaviour before calling backup acceptance complete. The same-drive limitation is explicit, not an extra cloud-setup release gate. Initial portal retention proposal:12 months, subject to storage measurement/owner confirmation; no automatic destructive cleanup. Keep approved previews unchanged and never use demo history/evidence in live outputs.

## 12. Build acceptance and boundaries

Implementation order and acceptance criteria live in the [execution specification](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md); current passed/open checks and actual report completion live in [Progress](../project/pj-tracker.md). Continue dependency-ready work across the [25 nonvisual requirements](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#23-whole-nonvisual-completion-tracker-and-finish-evidence). Local milestones and older downloaded packets do not establish current whole-pool research, updated reports or recurring acceptance. An unprocessed judgment is unfinished work, not an external access problem.

Before production prove:
- Real source relevance, enough eligible new concepts and supplier search within measured budgets.
- Each issue has >=10 products, >=5 of ten supported Matches each, the four combined fit/source priorities and <=20% meaningful updates.
- Category/identity/brand/safety policies work without unexplained over-filtering.
- Apify tests/production/unknown holds use the approved actual account periods and approximate pooled allowance. Minor cents do not cause approval pauses; no automatic paid upgrade or reset of old commitments.
- All planned dates, including partial/fifth weeks and provider-period boundaries, are covered.
- Functional PDF/Excel/portal facts, images and links agree; actual private upload/download bytes are verified and drafts cannot leak. Visual redesign remains separate.
- Approved scheduled releases work while the local computer is off; outages do not expose incomplete content.
- Interruption/resume, repeat publication, queued novelty, revision, backup/restore and cost-boundary tests pass.
- Owner has exact start/review/upload/recovery steps and confirms remaining setup choices.

Treat recurring volume/yield/cost forecasts as best-effort estimates. No six-month or fixed-yield guarantee is required to continue, but a small report must not be presented as proven recurring supply. If they materially conflict, save measured shortfall and discuss; no unapproved paid upgrade, extra allowance, weakened checklist or short publication. Minor cent-level variation is permitted under the cost policy.

Deferred/excluded: frontend/UI/UX/visual redesign, fully hosted research, client-triggered research, supplier outreach/verification, margin/RTO modelling, automatic purchasing, extra subscription services and multi-client productisation. Client publication still needs the actual approved recipient/package; routine public research/model work and private owner testing do not require another milestone approval.
