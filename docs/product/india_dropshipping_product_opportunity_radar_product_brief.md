## 1. Purpose and document ownership

NeuraFlow provides India-focused product research and possible supplier leads for one dropshipping/private-label client. The owner operates and reviews the service; the client reads and downloads approved reports through a private portal. Recommendations are opportunities worth testing, not sales, profit or RTO predictions.

Operate on a best-effort basis with normal research variation and reasonable labelled judgments. Approximate size/weight/handling inferred from actual form/mechanism is acceptable; do not demand measured packed weight or exact specifications for an ordinary sensible fit judgment. Label estimates, distinguish them from measurements and do not infer margin/RTO. Keep explicit delivery requirements, accurate source/accounting records and private access; do not require owner approval for routine searches, interpretation or verified intermediate milestones.

This brief owns product scope, selection, evidence and output requirements. The [cost plan](../operations/build_activation_and_costs_2026-10-03.md) owns budgets/forecast arithmetic; the [execution specification](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md) owns mechanisms/acceptance criteria; [Progress](../project/pj-tracker.md) owns current dated status; the [benchmark record](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/benchmark_record.md) retains historical measurements. ## 3. Product identity, brand judgment and continuity

Recommend a narrowly defined functional **type**, not one manufacturer's SKU and not an overly broad category. For example, “Adjustable doorway pull-up bar” is the opportunity; actual matching sellers/products/supplier offers sit underneath it. Preserve meaningful mechanism/mounting/use/age constraints. Ordinary colour, pack, seller wording or decorative finish is not a new recommendation. Multiple actual examples are desirable when useful, but two examples/two suppliers are not new eligibility gates. One substantiated example can be included honestly. Do not create a fictitious combined product by joining features from unrelated listings.

The existing concept identity already intends that functional meaning. Strengthen it with a versioned definition, manufacturer-independent report title and actual example/offer bindings, without replacing stable IDs or rewriting raw descriptions/history. Catalogue families remain research headings, not automatically recommendation identities. A broad heading may lead to several distinct narrow types. The implementation and historical merge/split/alias handling are in [section26.3](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#263-one-identity-explicit-meaning-and-actual-examples).

Assign one primary category and optional secondary tags. Home/Decor and Home/Kitchen overlap must not create duplicate products. Preserve canonical concept ID, aliases, product features and original source names.

Brand judgment is moderate, not a strict gate:
- Exclude recognisable established brand-specific products, such as Lakme, Hasbro or Meyer products.
- A named retailer, manufacturer name or unfamiliar small label does not itself disqualify a product.
- Accept reasonable generic/private-label candidates without demanding confirmed relabelling permission.
- Retain the judgment and reason, including borderline cases. A potential private-label candidate is not a confirmed supplier agreement.

The current AI description step supplies generic/small, established or unclear; code follows that judgment and does not automatically exclude an unfamiliar/unclear name. This is not a hard brand dictionary. Product versus generic service is a separate interpretation, and supplier e-commerce services are separate term fields. P2's explicit source-bound correction can restore a mistaken brand-only early exclusion before identity/scoring, including carrying its valid verdict into a new concept without overwriting an existing matched concept's judgment. Original answers/history remain; no routine second AI check is added. Automatic named brand-model dispatch remains later work in plan section21.18.

Exact duplicates merge; near matches use feature/function comparison. Different sizes/colours/sellers do not create new recommendations; meaningful functional, child/adult or powered/manual differences may. Ordinary supported AI judgments can be accepted after reference/feature checks. Only material unresolved ambiguity or published-history conflicts need targeted review; no manual approval of every semantic link. New status is computed from history after the identity decision, not guessed by AI.

“New” means never previously published to this client, not absent from only the last issue and not newly invented. Use all published history plus product allocations in already-approved upcoming issues. Allocate a concept to only one issue in a weekly batch by default. An update needs a material supported change and a short “what changed” reason; identical repeated evidence is not an update.

For actual issue size N: updates <= floor(N / 5), new >= N - floor(N / 5).
Examples: 10 => at least 8 new/at most 2 updates; 12 => 10/2; 15 => 12/3. No obligation to include updates. At ten per issue, three issues require 30 entries and at least 24 new products; with no eligible updates, all 30 must be new.

Maintain separate first-seen, first-published, last-observed and last-published dates. Drafts, benchmark products and manufactured preview data are not published history. Track queued allocations separately so preparing three issues together cannot double-count a product as new. If an earlier queued issue is cancelled, reconcile later allocations before publication.

## 4. Ten-criterion product fit

The active checklist has ten criteria. Retire original IDs **3 (High margin), 4 (Past winners) and 10 (Extremely unique)** from current scoring, reasons, selection and new client reports. Keep their historical records unchanged. Preserve the original IDs of the ten remaining criteria; do not renumber them or award points for retired criteria.

| Active score | Fit tier and report treatment |
|---|---|
| 6–10 Meets | **Good fit**; its place in a report follows the opportunity score (section 6). |
| 5 Meets | **Decent fit**; eligible under the same opportunity score rules. |
| 0–4 Meets, enough unknowns to potentially reach five | **More fit information needed**; not a proved product failure. Use a bounded practical judgment/review if useful. |
| 0–4 Meets, even all unknowns cannot reach five | Below the agreed fit threshold on current judgments. Keep the reasons; reopen only for a relevant change or named review. |

There is no minimum quota of Good-fit products. A useful ten-product issue can contain both tiers, or Decent-fit products alone when they satisfy the opportunity score rules below, identity, supplier-work and novelty requirements. Fit is suitability, not market performance or a promise of sales. The checklist is the score's "good product" part (25 of 100 points); demand, earliness and room decide the rest.

| Original ID / active criterion | Practical assessment guidance |
|---|---|
| 1. Small in size | Use known dimensions or described/visible form. An explained inference is sufficient; missing exact dimensions are not an automatic negative. |
| 2. Easy to ship | Consider likely handling, size, form, approximate weight and fragility. Reasonable labelled estimation is sufficient; no exact packed weight or shipping quote is required, and charges are not invented. |
| 5. Improves confidence | Identify a plausible product-specific confidence benefit, with its inferred basis. |
| 6. Improves convenience | Name the task made easier. Convenience need not also save time. |
| 7. Saves people time | Use a practical AI judgment about steps, handling or repeated work. A draining bowl may avoid transferring food into a separate sieve. Label the mechanism as inferred when not measured; do not invent minutes or a percentage. |
| 8. Solves a problem/fills a gap | Identify the actual problem/use case and how the product addresses it. |
| 9. Saves people money | Explain a plausible avoided expense or supported comparison. This concerns the customer, not seller margin. |
| 11. Improves quality of life | State a concrete everyday benefit without generic praise or medical promises. |
| 12. High perceived value | Assess usefulness, presentation or practical value; no profit estimate or exact margin is required. |
| 13. Women-centric audience | Use a moderate Indian-consumer positioning judgment, not a demand for measured gender percentages. Store women-centric, men-centric, unisex or unclear with a short reason. |

Each active criterion has **Meets, Does not meet, Unclear or Not assessed**, a reason, supplied source references where applicable, and observed/inferred/not-stated/conflicting basis. Code counts only supported Meets. Unclear and Not assessed contribute no point but are not negative evidence. Does not meet needs a supported practical reason; absence of proof alone is Unclear.

Audience describes likely buyers/users and product positioning, not what every man or woman does. A plain suitcase is ordinarily unisex; a beard-specific razor is ordinarily men-centric. An ordinary household kitchen aid may plausibly be women-centric in the client's Indian market without demographic statistics. Do not automatically mark every kitchen item women-centric, transfer a male positioning assumption to every nutrition product, or confuse a baby's functional user with the purchaser. Reasonable product-specific inference can earn the women-centric point; supported men-centric/unisex positioning normally does not earn that point, but **does not exclude the product**. Unclear remains unknown. Buyer positioning stays separate from functional identity.

For time-saving, ask the AI to name the ordinary task, likely changed step and plausible saving mechanism, or explain no plausible saving/unknown. Do not add a stopwatch, measured-data requirement or another model call per criterion. A “ten units before, fewer after” example is optional explanatory illustration only, clearly labelled hypothetical; it is never a measured value, market signal or basis for arithmetic points. The normal report uses a short phrase such as “Fewer transfers while draining.”

Overlapping benefits can count separately when each has a sensible product-specific explanation. Do not require documentary proof for obvious practical benefits or add a confidence-percentage gate. A named review can raise or lower judgments, keeps the original result, and is reused for unchanged inputs rather than repeated until the score passes.

Implementation contracts: [ten-criterion migration](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#2116-ten-criterion-fit-policy-and-coordinated-migration), [source priorities](../operations/benchmarks/2026-10-03/execution_specification.md#24-n03-current-source-support-and-n25-record-recovery-contracts), and [practical answer ownership](../operations/implementation_2026-10-11.md#r03-practical-judgments-and-exact-answer-ownership). Historical jobs and packets keep their original meaning/bytes; current assessment counts belong to Progress.

## 6. Market assessment and selection

Keep market evidence separate from the ten-criterion fit and the Good/Decent tier. Since 15 October 2026 the owner's **opportunity score** out of 100 (`opportunity-score-v3`) replaces the four priority tiers and their site counts. Code measures it from saved facts only; no model opinion enters it (the AI still judges whether each listing, ad or chart is the same product). The owner set the four part weights; the inner points live in a versioned settings file (`config/research/opportunity_score.json`).

| Part | Points | What earns them |
|---|---:|---|
| Do people want it? | 20 | Facebook ads up to 8 (Meta positive with 5+ relevant advertisers 8; Meta positive 6; relevant ads but not positive 2). Google searches up to 5 (usable India 12-month chart with 4+ nonzero points 5; 1–3 nonzero points 2). Dropshipping platforms' lists up to 5 (a relevant item on a bestseller / trending / high-demand list of 2+ platforms 5, of one 4; otherwise listed on 2+ platforms 2, on one 1). Quick-commerce stock up to 2 (a relevant in-stock listing in 2+ cities 2, in one 1). Amazon and Flipkart give no want points. |
| Is it early? | 30 | Rising searches up to 12 (rising by the rule below 8; also rising by 50% or more on the lower ratio bound, or a same-product "Breakout" rising query, 12). New advertisers up to 9 (advertisers whose first relevant ad started within 30 days of being seen: 3+ = 9, 2 = 6, 1 = 3). Newly launched lists up to 9 (a relevant item on the new-arrivals list of 2+ platforms 9, of one 6). |
| Room for us? | 25 | Amazon/Flipkart crowding Low 25, Medium 12, High 0. Not checked earns nothing and blocks inclusion. |
| Good product? | 25 | Ten-point checklist: 5 Meets = 10 points, each further Meet +3 (10 Meets = 25). |

Measurements the score reuses (unchanged definitions):
- Meta positive: at least three distinct relevant advertisers OR at least five relevant active ads with oldest relevant active ad at least 14 days old.
- Trends: consistent India 12-month series; exclude incomplete periods; at least four nonzero points. Compare last four comparable periods with preceding four; rising requires ratio >= 1.15 and increase >= five points (on the bounds of any "<1" values). Supported same-product rising related queries may satisfy the alternative rule. With zero previous average, require at least two recent nonzero periods plus a supporting rising query; otherwise insufficient/stable as appropriate. Missing, sparse or all-zero data earns no points; it is never negative demand.
- Crowding: section 5 and the crowding check (Low / Medium / High from reviewed relevant listings; Not checked is never Low).
- Quick-commerce and platform facts count only relevant reviewed items; a platform presence fact older than a week earns nothing.

Labels shown in reports: **Too quiet** (under 5 want points; not reportable), **Proven but crowded** (High crowding), **Early bet** (Low or Medium crowding, early 15+ and want under 10), **Sweet spot** (every other Low or Medium type). Reports show "Opportunity score NN/100" with the four parts and the label, and "Amazon/Flipkart crowding: Low / Medium / High" with the counts behind it. Every sub-point is stored with its facts and evidence ids, and every reader re-checks the arithmetic and label.

Selection order:
1. Check identity/category, moderate brand decision and lightweight safety flags.
2. Inclusion needs at least five supported Matches across the ten active criteria (practical observed/inferred reasons; unknowns are not failures; complete their actual assessment rather than presume negative results), at least 5 of the 20 want points, and a known crowding level. Otherwise the type waits with a named reason (`fit_information_pending`, `crowding_pending`, `demand_too_quiet`); waiting is not rejection.
3. In each issue, Low and Medium crowding types go first, highest score first. Inside a crowding group the order is: higher score, then higher early, then higher product points; only types still equal after that are separated by category balance (a category with fewer than two types already in the issue first, then the category with fewer types in it) and the earlier usable-until date; the stable type id decides last. The issue's New/Updated mix and its own held types are settled before this order (item 4). High crowding types are used only when fewer than ten Low/Medium types are ready for that issue: Low/Medium types already in it, plus those it can still actually take (New ones, and Updated ones only within the update cap). Suppliers never add points.
4. Preserve each issue's size, required New share and holds; category balance does not justify weak padding.
5. Record actual discouraging information and obvious unsuitable products with a specific reason. An old label alone is not a completed suitability judgment.
6. Allocate products to issues, enforce each issue's size and novelty, perform supplier search and validate the package.
7. If insufficient eligible products remain, save exact shortfall/rejection counts and ask the owner. Continue independent work; do not auto-publish a short report.

One-time calibration, exactly once in total (a later settings version never starts another): the sample is the first 50 scored types (5+ Meets and a known crowding level) in the order they were first scored, each with the label it had then; if more than 70% or fewer than 10% of them are Too quiet, the agent adjusts only the inner point thresholds once (never the owner's four weights), with a new settings version, records the before/after counts and reports it. The owner meter and the desktop app show scored types per label and the calibration alert.

Movement comparisons require comparable source sets and collection/query/assessment policies. A changed policy or coverage is not strengthened demand. Reusing the same observation across issue dates is not a new trend measurement.

## 7. Supplier search and recurring-fee preference

Cursory discovery is required for every recommended product. Aim for 1–3 useful leads, up to five when easily available; zero substantiated leads is acceptable after actual recorded effort. This includes genuinely attempted searches/routes that fail or produce no usable pages/interpretation. An otherwise eligible product can appear with **“Supplier search did not produce any usable results.”** Keep exact empty/blocked/error/pending details internally; do not claim the product/supply is unavailable or no suppliers exist. Preserve useful subsequent follow-up without holding the product indefinitely. Prepared links or prelaunch-only checks are not actual supplier effort.

Search direct Indian manufacturers/wholesalers/importers, IndiaMART (read in the program's signed-in research browser, reading only), TradeIndia, Google Maps (read directly in the program's own browser) and the four dropshipping platforms (a relevant listing is a supplier lead with the platform's price and stated dropship terms). IndiaMART and TradeIndia listings found by wider discovery are labelled supplier leads in reports, never market examples. Google Search supplier results are retired from automatic research (owner decision, 14 October 2026): Google requires JavaScript for automated reading, there is no approved official route, and Google is neither bypassed nor replaced by a paid search service. Saved Google Search records stay as history; they are not current supplier effort and no product waits on them. Owner reports permission for the proposed Maps supplier/manufacturer discovery. Signed-in sites never pay, never accept a plan or membership prompt, and never click contact, "View number", "Get best price" or "Send enquiry"; a plan wall makes that route wait. Do not bypass CAPTCHA/access controls; record blocks and use a permitted alternative or guided manual step. Do not contact suppliers, request quotes, buy access or create accounts automatically.

For each lead record:
- Candidate/concept ID; supplier name, type, location when supported.
- Product/company page URL, retrieval status, timestamp and evidence excerpt/reference.
- Match type: exact/close product or broader relevant manufacturer/company lead.
- Supply arrangement: single-piece purchase with direct customer dispatch; bulk stock purchase followed by individual dispatch; bulk supply only; or Not stated. Record the evidence for each part separately; “MOQ 1” alone does not establish dropshipping.
- Minimum purchase quantity and any stock prepayment/deposit, where stated. Hundreds of separate single-item customer orders are not one bulk purchase.
- E-commerce support as separate stated/unknown fields: direct dispatch, COD, store/order integration, neutral packaging and private-label services. “E-commerce support” alone establishes none of these.
- Private-label support: stated yes, stated no, or unknown; not mandatory for retaining a plausible lead.
- Subscription status: No subscription required — stated; Subscription required; Optional paid plan; Not stated.
- Mandatory fee amount/frequency where stated; separate setup fee, deposit, MOQ and per-order charge fields.
- Short suitability/limitation note.

Check readily accessible pricing/FAQ/reseller/dropshipping pages. No pricing page means Not stated, not no fee. Free signup does not prove free fulfilment access. Consider recurring annual plans too, not only monthly subscriptions; optional paid tiers must be genuinely optional for the proposed workflow.

Among relevant leads, prefer a usable no-subscription route. Unknown terms do not exclude a plausible supplier; mandatory-subscription suppliers remain lower-priority alternatives. Product relevance and fulfilment usefulness still matter. Do not rank an irrelevant “free” supplier first. Reuse dated supplier-level terms across related leads; distinguish platform membership, buyer access and seller advertising charges.

Supplier discovery must progress beyond basic first-hit links: seek useful Indian product/company leads, read readily available terms, and record the real queries/fetched or blocked pages. An empty completed search is valid; actually attempted searches with no usable result are also sufficient best-effort work for inclusion with the limitation. AI keys also include relevant meaning/evidence/model/prompt/assessment version, while presentation-only changes do not reset identity. Unknown fields/charges stay unknown, never zero. Use decimal money; persist reservations before charged requests and retain unknown-cost holds. Prevent overlapping charged work, reconcile uncertain submissions before retrying, and bound all network/process work. Paid GETs are not safe automatic retries.
